# ADR 034 - Email vs Slack for Notifications

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The pipeline needed a notification strategy for two distinct audiences: on-call engineers who need
immediate awareness of anomalies and failures, and business stakeholders who need a daily digest
of pipeline health. A single channel wouldn't serve both well.

## Decision
Use both — Slack for real-time alerts and email for daily reports. Slack handles time-sensitive
events (anomalies, failures, quality warnings) while email delivers formatted HTML daily reports
via SMTP.

## Reasons
- Slack gives immediate visibility to on-call engineers with color-coded severity
- Email provides a formal daily report for stakeholders — archivable and printable
- Both have graceful fallbacks (return False) if not configured — pipeline never breaks
- Slack color coding (red/yellow/green) makes severity immediately visible in the feed
- Email HTML reports are generated by Jinja2 and stored in S3 for audit trail
- Separating channels keeps engineer alerts out of stakeholder inboxes

## Consequences
- Two notification systems to maintain — Slack webhook + SMTP credentials in `.env`
- Engineers must configure both if they want full coverage
- Free Slack has a 10k message history limit — not a concern at 20 messages/day
- Future: consolidate to PagerDuty for production on-call rotation
